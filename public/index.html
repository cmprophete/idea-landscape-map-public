How it works

  1. 01StatementWrite out the idea or claim. It doesn't have to be perfect.
  2. 02ClarifyAnswer up to four questions so the claim is precise.
  3. 03ClaimPick one of three versions, or edit it.
  4. 04PeopleStart from the key voices in the debate, then add or remove names.
  5. 05ResearchClaude reads each person's own words, and every quote is checked against its source.

Each step uses Claude, Anthropic's AI model. It suggests the questions and claim versions, searches the web for people and what they've said, places each person, and writes the summary. The quote checks are done separately by the app's own code, which reopens each source page.

What to keep in mind

  • Built with ClaudeThis app is built and run with Claude, Anthropic's AI model, which can make mistakes. Read the quotes and open the linked sources before you rely on a placement.
  • UsageUsage depends on your available credits and tokens. Most of it goes to research, so each map covers up to 12 people and stays within its research budget.
  • A snapshot in timeEach map shows what people had said as of its research date. Perspectives change, so a placement can go out of date. Run a map again to see what's shifted.
  • Who gets foundThe app finds people through what they've published or said on the record online: articles, papers, interviews, and posts. Community members whose views carry real weight but who have little media presence, such as organizers, workers, and local leaders, can be missing. You can add names on the People step, but the app can only place someone whose words it can find and check.
  • Sources it can't readSome sites block automated reading, including several major newspapers and social platforms. Recent statements published there may be missing.
